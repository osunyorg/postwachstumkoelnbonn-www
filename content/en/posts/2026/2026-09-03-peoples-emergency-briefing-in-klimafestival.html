Wednesday 23 September 19:45-21:30
Come and see this 50-minute campaign film from the UK and share your reactions. We are screening the British film ‘People’s Emergency Briefing’ (with German subtitles) during the Klimafestival Köln as an example of climate campaigning from the UK. The 50-minute film has so far been shown exclusively at in-person screenings to bring people and politicians together. Since April this year, over 3000 events have been organised. We will be asking for donations to cover the venue costs.  Venue: Motoki Kollektiv e.V., Stammstraße 32-34, 50823, Ehrenfeld.   For more information on the Klimafestival   For more information on the film see https://www.nebriefing.org/
