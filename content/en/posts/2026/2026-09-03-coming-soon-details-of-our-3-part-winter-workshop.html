After two successful workshops, we are running our first course, exploring post-growth ideas through activities, short texts and local case studies.

This is a chance to spark your imagination, reflect on deep changes in simple ways, and discover local projects.

We will be using short exercises, either solo, in pairs or small groups, and will have plenty of pauses to reflect or chat. There will be small bits of 'homework' to prepare for each session, that can be done in under an hour, or more if you want. 

Venue: A housing co-operative on Rolshoverstr, Kalk. Nearest stations: Kalk Post/Trimbornstr

There are limited places - please apply (external link) by Sunday 27th September - we aim to respond within one week. We look forward to hearing from you!
