If you would like to support the promotion of post-growth ideas and approaches in the Cologne-Bonn region please contact us (see below) .
 
We don't currently have regular meetings but we have a Signal group, and smaller groups that meet in person or online to organise our events.
 
We welcome people that would like to get involved in organising these events and activities. We are interested and open to hearing your ideas to promote post-growth, and we hope you can find like-minded people in our group to work together with.

Our events
Workshops
We have run workshops in Cologne and Bonn, and have now planned a course for winter 2026. Please see our News page! Please apply by 27th September.   
To find out more - contact us  -  see below.

Film screenings
 Together with the organisation Foyer Futur, we organised two degrowth film evenings in October 2025, followed by a discussion with the director. We are grateful for your donations which helped to cover expenses, and are planning another screening in September 2026. If you want to suggest venues for future events, please get in touch! To find out more - contact us  -  see below.

Reading circle
One member suggests a short text such as an article or chapter of a book. Then we meet to share what stood out to us. Each person tends to bring their own experience and perspective and notices different things. This way we get to hear different views and get to know each other better.  To find out more - contact us -  see below.

Other events

Another likeminded group to follow in Cologne is Foyer Futur e.V. - events in German language. Further information
